News

My first-ever submitted paper got accepted. That gave me the wrong impression of academia: you do research, write about it, publish it. Nice. Usually a paper or proposal is rejected, modified, re-submitted, and sometimes (rarely, I think/hope) stays unpublished. Seeing that it's not all about acceptance, but also about trying, is useful for me and hopefully for others, too.

This log starts in September 2026.
