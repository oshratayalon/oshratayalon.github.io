News

My first-ever submitted paper was to SOUPS in 2013 — and it got accepted. That gave me the wrong impression of academia: you do research, write about it, and publish it. Nice.

The truth is that in many cases your paper/proposal/etc. is rejected, modified, re-submitted, and sometimes (rarely, I think) will stay unpublished. Seeing that it's not all about acceptance, but also about trying, is useful for me and hopefully for others, too.

This log starts in September 2026.

  • September 2026. Desk reject from Online Information Review for "From Regulation to Engineering: Organizational Privacy Climate as Mediator".
  • September 2026. Proposal to the 2026–2027 Research Grants in Child and Youth Rights declined.
