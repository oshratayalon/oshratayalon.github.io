About Me

Hello! I'm the head of the Society and Technology Interaction Lab (SoTI Lab) at the Department of Information Systems, University of Haifa, Israel.

My main research interests are human–computer interaction and usable privacy and security. I enjoy exploring topics that examine technology, people, how they interact, and how we can improve technology to advance society.

In particular, I'm interested in exploring usable privacy and security, and online safety more broadly, among specific populations, such as children and adolescents. Visit the people page to meet the team.

A full list of my publications is on Google Scholar and DBLP.
